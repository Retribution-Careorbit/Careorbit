## WHAT TO REMOVE / AVOID

- ❌ No gradient backgrounds on entire sidebar sections
- ❌ No mixing of neon gradients with pastel card backgrounds 
- ❌ No card borders that use multiple colors on different sides randomly
- ❌ No heavy shadows that create fake depth inconsistently
- ❌ No all-caps names in greeting (ARJYA → Arjya)
- ❌ No inconsistent icon sizes (standardize: 16px nav, 18px actions, 20px card headers)
- ❌ No browser-default form inputs — style all inputs uniformly
- ❌ No content that starts at different X-positions across pages
